## Why

Automated tests cannot prove connector reauthorization, a Google-issued DPoP-bound refresh token, browser consent usability, or seven days of production behavior. This task owns those integrated gates and keeps operator/time evidence distinct from implementation evidence.

## Scope

Stable 1.0.0 product/server identity; migration rehearsal and rollback; production schema counts; historical 0.2.1 and 0.3.0 deploy evidence; ChatGPT, Claude.ai, Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor authorization/discovery/ping/live-read checks; work-workspace Claude propagation; Google prompt-consent DPoP replacement and refresh; responsive/accessibility/font QA; logs, latency, protocol-era and Neon-query monitoring; seven-day legacy-table retention and cleanup. The separate real Fitbit webhook delivery gate remains owned by `#w11/#q11`.

## Plan

Promote the package and MCP implementation identity to 1.0.0 while leaving the protocol SDK at
2.0.0 and preserving the icon metadata. Retain the historical
[`0.3.0` cutover runbook](../../docs/operations/0.3.0-cutover.md) and receipts. Pause for fresh
approval before connector revocation/removal, Google reconsent, or destructive cleanup. Establish
a DPoP-capable rollback artifact before reconsent; monitor 24 hours and seven days; delete legacy
auth rows/tables only after zero required rollback use and explicit acceptance.

## Impact

The stable release reports 1.0.0 without changing tools, resources, schemas, auth behavior, endpoint URLs, or Health behavior. Completing the remaining qualification makes 1.0.0 the sole forward path and removes the legacy OAuth rollback store; until then, rollback remains available through the recorded artifacts.

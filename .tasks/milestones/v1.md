TT;DR: Get Emmett's personal Google Health MCP server built, deployed on Vercel, and answering real health questions from Claude and ChatGPT — with nutrition/measurement writes — by mid-July.

## Why
Emmett wears a Fitbit Air and wants any of his LLM assistants (Claude Code/Desktop, claude.ai web+mobile, ChatGPT) to answer questions like "how many steps today?", "how did I sleep?", "why am I tired?", "what did I eat yesterday?" from real synced data, and to log nutrition/hydration/measurements the device can't track. Full design + decisions: `docs/PLAN.md` (overrides the ChatGPT handoff spec where they conflict).

## Scope
IN: Next.js on Vercel, better-auth OAuth 2.1 + DCR (Google-federated, allowlisted), Google Health consent flow with AES-256-GCM encrypted tokens in Neon, Google Health API client + data-type registry, 9 read tools + 5 resources + 5 write tools, audit logging, tests, deploy + real-client E2E.
OUT (v1): webhooks (v1.1 = #w11 in Backlog), sleep/exercise/settings writes, caching beyond profile/settings TTL, analytics/warehouse features, medical reasoning.
